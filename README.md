# 🎬 The Godfather

**The Godfather** is my favorite movie. It’s an American crime film directed by *Francis Ford Coppola* and based on the novel by *Mario Puzo*.

## 👨‍👩‍👦 Main Characters
- **Don Vito Corleone (Marlon Brando)** — The powerful and respected head of the Corleone family.  
- **Michael Corleone (Al Pacino)** — Don Vito’s youngest son, who gradually takes over the family business.  
- **Sonny Corleone (James Caan)** — The hot-headed eldest son.  
- **Tom Hagen (Robert Duvall)** — The family’s trusted consigliere (advisor).

## 📍 Setting
The story takes place in **New York City** and **Sicily** during the 1940s and 1950s.

## 💭 Why I Like It
> “It’s a timeless story about power, loyalty, and family — with unforgettable performances and masterful direction.”

### 🎞️ Favorite Scene
`The restaurant scene where Michael takes his first decisive step into the world of crime.`

### 🧠 Fun Fact
The famous line *“I’m gonna make him an offer he can’t refuse”* is ranked among the most iconic movie quotes of all time.
